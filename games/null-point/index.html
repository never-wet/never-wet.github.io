How to play

Start on the green Main Baseplate. Your free first upgrade produces P$ automatically.

Buy the next visible pad. Some gold pads open completely new colored baseplates. New-area pads deliberately have no long connector line.

Use the Clicker area for active income. Alpha and Omega add larger automatic generators. Prestige and Evil reset earlier progress for permanent currencies and multipliers.

Your progress saves in this browser and earns up to four hours of offline P$.
